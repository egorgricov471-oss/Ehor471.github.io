The War in Ukraine

The Russo-Ukrainian War is an unprovoked war of aggression waged by the Russian Federation against sovereign Ukraine, ongoing since February 2014, which has become the largest armed conflict in Europe since World War II.

For Ukraine, this is an existential war for survival, the preservation of its statehood, national identity, and the right to independently choose its own future. For Russia, this aggression is an imperial attempt to destroy Ukrainian statehood, seize territories, and reshape the global order by force.

Beginning with the hybrid occupation of Crimea and parts of Donbas in 2014, it escalated into a full-scale invasion on February 24, 2022, where modern technologies and drones merged with brutal positional attrition warfare
