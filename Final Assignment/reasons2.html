The reasons of the War

Imperial Ambitions
Russia never accepted the collapse of the USSR and seeks to restore its empire by absorbing Ukraine
Nuclear Disarmament
In 1994, Ukraine gave up the world's third-largest nuclear arsenal for security assurances that Russia later violated
European Choice
Ukrainians' aspiration to join the EU and NATO shattered Moscow's plans to keep Kyiv in its sphere of influence
Fear of Democracy
A free and democratic Ukraine posed a direct threat to Putin's authoritarian regime as an inspiring example for Russians
Denial of Identity
Russian propaganda denied the existence of a separate Ukrainian nation, language, and history to justify assimilation
Weak Western Response
Mild sanctions after the 2008 invasion of Georgia and the 2014 annexation of Crimea convinced the Kremlin of its impunity
Strategic Resources
Control over Ukraine's fertile black soil, ports, energy reserves, and industrial potential strengthens Russia's geopolitical power
Reshaping World Order
Russia used the invasion to blackmail the West and prove that state borders can be redrawn by military force
